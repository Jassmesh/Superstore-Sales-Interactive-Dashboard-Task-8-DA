Sales Dashboard – Key Insights

1. The West region consistently led in sales across all quarters, peaking in November.
2. Technology category outperformed Furniture and Office Supplies in both sales and profit margins.
3. Sales showed a steady upward trend during Q4, indicating strong end-of-year performance.
4. The South region had relatively lower sales and profitability, suggesting potential for targeted marketing.
5. November recorded the highest monthly sales overall, likely driven by seasonal promotions.
